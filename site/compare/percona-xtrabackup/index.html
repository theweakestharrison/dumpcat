DumpCat vs Percona XtraBackup

Percona XtraBackup is the standard open-source tool for hot physical MySQL backups, and DumpCat uses it. On its own it is a command-line engine: scheduling, retention, uploads, binlogs and alerts are left to your scripts. This page shows what DumpCat adds on top, and when the bare tool is enough.

Choose Percona XtraBackup if

  • You already have reliable scripts, monitoring and runbooks around XtraBackup and only need the engine.
  • You need an XtraBackup option that DumpCat does not expose, or a fully custom pipeline.

Choose DumpCat if

  • You want XtraBackup and MariaBackup scheduled from a web UI, with full + incremental chains and retention handled for you.
  • You want binlogs archived continuously, so a physical backup plus binlogs restores to any second.
  • You want age encryption, uploads to S3, Google Drive, SFTP and more, and alerts when something fails.
  • You want restores that download the full + incremental chain, prepare it and can replace the datadir for you, rolling back automatically if any step fails.

Switching from Percona XtraBackup

  1. Install DumpCat on the database server; the Linux installer installs the XtraBackup or MariaBackup version that matches your server.
  2. Create a job with the physical method, a full schedule and an incremental schedule.
  3. Turn on binlog archiving for point-in-time recovery, run the job once, then retire your cron scripts.

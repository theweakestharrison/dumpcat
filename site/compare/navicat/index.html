DumpCat vs Navicat

Navicat for MySQL is a full database client from PremiumSoft, and backup is one of its many features. DumpCat does only backups and restores, as a service on the server. If you mostly use Navicat to schedule backups, this is how the two compare.

Choose Navicat if

  • You want a full database IDE for queries, modelling and data transfer, and occasional backups are a bonus.
  • You back up small databases by hand from your workstation.

Choose DumpCat if

  • You want backups to run on the server, not on a desktop that has to stay on.
  • You want backups compressed, encrypted and copied to S3, Google Drive, OneDrive or SFTP automatically.
  • You need retention, alerts on Telegram, Slack or Discord, and point-in-time recovery.
  • You want hot physical backups with XtraBackup / MariaBackup for large databases. And it is free.
